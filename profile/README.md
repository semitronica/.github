### 🚀 About Us

Based in Vila Nova de Famalicão, Portugal, **Semitrónica** is an engineering powerhouse providing end-to-end R&D collaboration and in-house manufacturing. With a history spanning over three decades, we partner with industry leaders across the globe to break down the barriers of hardware and software development, delivering scalable, future-proof microelectronics and IoT solutions.

### 🌐 [End-to-End R&D IoT Program](https://semitronica.pt/iot-program)

We disrupt traditional DIY development models by offering a comprehensive support program where hardware and software are designed as a unified system. You retain full ownership of all engineering materials while we handle the heavy lifting:

*   **Sensor Technology & Integration:** Custom engineering of advanced sensing technology (mechanical, environmental, electrochemical) tailored to your industry requirements, alongside optimized antenna design.
*   **Cloud Services & APIs:** Infinite scalability with 99.9%+ uptime. Our cloud architecture includes production-ready MLOps platforms, seamless Over-The-Air (OTA) device management, and secure API gateways with no vendor lock-in.
*   **Mobile Apps:** Development of intuitive, secure, cross-platform (iOS/Android) mobile interfaces that empower users to interact seamlessly with their connected devices.
*   **Cellular Connectivity:** Global management of cellular channels, connecting you with top MNO partners for optimal global coverage and competitive costs.

### 🏭 Electronic Manufacturing Services (EMS)

Our state-of-the-art factory guarantees the accuracy, reliability, and long-term performance of your devices—from early prototyping to full-scale mass production:
*   **PCB Assembly:** Comprehensive SMT and THT manufacturing capabilities.
*   **Rigorous Testing:** Automated Optical Inspection (AOI), In-Circuit Testing (ICT), alongside full functional and integration testing.
*   **Certification Support:** Expert guidance to achieve CE certification, including EMC and LVD testing.

### 💡 Proprietary Technologies

We also develop standalone advanced solutions that showcase our technical depth:
*   **[iON: Intelligent Electricity](https://ionelectricity.com/):** Home Energy Management Systems (EMS).
*   **[Asense](https://semitronica.pt/asense/):** Wireless Structural Health Monitoring (SHM).
